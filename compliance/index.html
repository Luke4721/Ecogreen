Statutory Compliance

E-Waste (Management) Rules Compliance Statement

Unbroken regulatory passbooks protecting corporate directors from vicarious liability.

EcoGreen operates strictly under the regulatory mandates established by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) and Central Pollution Control Board. Every consignment ingested at our facilities generates statutory Form 6 transit manifests and digital Form 2 inventory passbook credits.

We maintain real-time automated synchronization with the CPCB EPR portal, guaranteeing that all producer responsibility obligations are fulfilled legally, digitally, and irrevocably.

Regulatory Guidance

Compliance FAQs for Corporate Teams

Answers to standard legal and procurement due diligence inquiries.

How do you issue Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credits? +
As a CPCB-registered recycler, we verify received quantities, process material through authorized recycling lines, and generate digital EPR credits directly on the CPCB centralized EPR portal for our corporate partners.
What statutory returns and forms are provided to corporate clients? +
Clients receive signed Form 6 transit manifests for every shipment, Form 2 inventory passbooks, and annual statutory reconciliation certificates that satisfy state and central pollution control boards without follow-up queries.
How long are serial records and certificates archived? +
All serial-level sanitization records, weighbridge logs, and Certificates of Destruction are retained in secure immutable cloud archives for seven years, accessible on demand during corporate internal audits.
What is the frequency of your third-party environmental audits? +
Our facilities undergo annual surveillance audits by accredited ISO certification registrars, semi-annual inspections by state pollution control boards, and quarterly unannounced audits by independent environmental compliance firms.
