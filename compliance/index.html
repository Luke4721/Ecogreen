Statutory compliance

E-Waste (Management) Rules compliance statement.

EcoGreen operates in strict compliance with the statutory provisions of the E-Waste (Management) Rules 2016 and subsequent amendments promulgated by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). All electronic scrap consignments are received under serialized Form 6 transport manifests, ensuring end-to-end chain of custody from corporate loading docks to our authorized recycling bays.

We maintain digital record passbooks in Form 2, submit statutory annual returns under Form 3, and provide our enterprise clients with verified documentation required for statutory reporting and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credit settlement on the CPCB portal.

Oversight

Downstream audit policy and vendor governance.

Zero landfill compliance requires rigorous auditing across every participant in our secondary material ecosystem. EcoGreen conducts unannounced on-site audits of all authorized downstream refiners, secondary smelters, and polymer re-compounders.

Downstream partners are verified for effluent treatment plant operation, workplace safety protocols, and manifest tracking. Any discrepancy or unauthorized transfer to uncertified subcontractors triggers immediate commercial disqualification and statutory reporting.

Compliance inquiries

Statutory & regulatory FAQs.

How do you issue Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credits?
As a CPCB-registered recycler, we verify received quantities, process material through authorized recycling lines, and generate digital EPR credits directly on the CPCB centralized EPR portal for our corporate partners.
What statutory returns and forms are provided to corporate clients?
Clients receive signed Form 6 transit manifests for every shipment, Form 2 inventory passbooks, and annual statutory reconciliation certificates that satisfy state and central pollution control boards without follow-up queries.
How long are serial records and certificates archived?
All serial-level sanitization records, weighbridge logs, and Certificates of Destruction are retained in secure immutable cloud archives for seven years, accessible on demand during corporate internal audits.
What is the frequency of your third-party environmental audits?
Our facilities undergo annual surveillance audits by accredited ISO certification registrars, semi-annual inspections by state pollution control boards, and quarterly unannounced audits by independent environmental compliance firms.
