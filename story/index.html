Our Milestones

10 Years

At EcoGreen, excellence and innovation are not just goals;
they are the very essence of our culture.

Our commitment to fostering a sustainable circular economy empowers our teams to push boundaries, innovate closed-loop extraction methods, and deliver verified chain-of-custody tracking. Together, we are shaping the future of Indian material security with passion, dedication, and a relentless pursuit of environmental excellence.

2015

Company was established in Greater Noida with our first Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) authorized dismantling and material sorting line.

2017

Achieved ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications. Pioneered mechanized circuit board separation for corporate telecom and enterprise hardware.

2018

Commissioned proprietary hydrometallurgical refining line, achieving 99.5% high-purity precious metal extraction (gold, silver, palladium, copper).

2019

Expanded operations across Southern India with authorized processing hubs in Bengaluru and Hyderabad to service hyperscale data centers.

2020

Deployed military-grade NIST 800-88 compliant 3-pass cryptographic data sanitization suites across on-site and mobile ITAD engineering teams.

2021

Launched RecyCole – our circular recovery and e-waste collection initiative, the first of its kind in India.

2022

Crossed 35,000 MT of cumulatively processed electronic assets and onboarded over 150 leading banking, insurance, and telecommunications enterprises.

2023

All eligible processing plants got GreenCo certified (3 Gold ratings and 2 Silver ratings) under Confederation of Indian Industry environmental audits.

2024

Launched automated digital chain-of-custody tracking platform and statutory CPCB Form 6 compliance portal for enterprise corporate clients.

2025

Crossed 60,000 MT milestone. Certified secure with international TISAX® level information security compliance for enterprise hardware.

2026

A Decade of Operating History. Expanding specialized lithium-ion battery and solar PV module recovery lines across 6 authorized facilities nationwide.

Operating Discipline

Building India's Sovereign Raw Material Reserve

Every kilogram of recycled circuit boards and decommissioned server arrays directly reduces national dependence on imported rare metals and mined mineral ores.

By combining strict compliance with military-grade data purge methods, EcoGreen guarantees that circular stewardship never compromises data confidentiality or regulatory integrity.
