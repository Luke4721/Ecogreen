The GreenCo Journey at EcoGreen Recyclers

At EcoGreen, sustainability is not a post-script — it is engineered into our chemical reactors, rooftop solar microgrids, and mechanical separation lines.

Under rigorous audits by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) – Sohrabji Godrej Green Business Centre, all five of our eligible processing plants have achieved GreenCo certification (3 Gold ratings and 2 Silver ratings), validating our performance across greenhouse gas mitigation, zero liquid discharge, and renewable energy adoption.

Operating Creed

Compete, Collaborate and Excel

The three foundational principles that guide our environmental leadership and customer partnerships.

PILLAR 01

Compete

We empower Indian manufacturers to compete on the global stage with lower Scope 3 embodied carbon. By providing high-purity recycled copper ingots and refined secondary precious metals, we reduce reliance on volatile foreign supply chains.

PILLAR 02

Collaborate

We collaborate deeply with client IT teams, state pollution control boards, and community organizations. Through nationwide awareness and direct pickup initiatives like RecyCole, we channel informal e-waste into certified closed loops.

PILLAR 03

Excel

Excellence is our permanent operating condition. Through continuous research in non-thermal hydrometallurgical chemistry, automated robotic sorting, and serialized data security, we redefine the standard for responsible industrial recycling.
