Resource Philosophy

Closing the Technological Mineral Cycle

Urban mining is the only thermodynamically rational future for strategic mineral security.

The linear economic model of extraction, manufacture, consumption, and disposal has pushed global mineral supply chains to their physical boundaries. Critical transition elements—such as cobalt, lithium, nickel, and copper—are finite, geopolitically vulnerable, and environmentally catastrophic to extract from primary ore bodies.

“Urban mining is not an alternative to primary extraction. It is the only thermodynamically rational future for strategic mineral security.”

By processing complex electronics through closed-loop hydrometallurgical pipelines rather than high-emission pyrometallurgical smelters, EcoGreen recovers essential materials with up to 85% less energy consumption than virgin mining. Every gram of copper recovered is a gram that does not need to be blasted from a hillside.
