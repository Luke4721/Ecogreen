WELCOME TO ECOGREEN

Advanced Closed-Loop Material Recovery & ITAD for Critical Applications

Founded in 2015, EcoGreen Recyclers (P) Limited is a statutory leader in end-to-end IT Asset Disposition (ITAD), high-purity non-ferrous metallurgical refining, and circular electronics manufacturing feed. Operating across 5 major industrial corridors, we secure India's closed-loop lifecycle from hardware decommissioning to domestic remanufacturing.
