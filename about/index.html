Company Overview

Making Circularity In Electronic Engineering A Reality

Founded in 2015, EcoGreen is India's statutory leader in certified e-waste recycling and IT asset disposition. We operate an integrated network of authorized processing plants bridging high-volume corporate refresh cycles with domestic raw material security.

Enterprise Security Meets Environmental Stewardship

Operating across Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Telangana, we provide enterprise organizations with auditable chain-of-custody tracking, cryptographic data destruction, and manufacturing-grade hydrometallurgical mineral recovery.

We operate under zero-landfill mandates, converting discarded printed circuit boards, high-density storage arrays, and lithium battery cells into strategic industrial inputs for Indian manufacturers.

Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision, and Operational Values

The principles that govern our engineers across every facility floor and loading dock.

01 / MISSION

Our Mission

To make electronic waste a strategic domestic resource rather than an environmental liability — through closed-loop recovery processes that safeguard enterprise data security and protect our natural ecosystems.

02 / VISION

Our Vision

A zero-landfill India where every end-of-life computing asset, server blade, and telecommunication switch returns 100% of its critical minerals and precious metals back into domestic manufacturing lines.

03 / VALUES

Operational Values

  • Integrity: Unbroken serial chain of custody verified at every weighbridge intake.
  • Transparency: Audited recovery manifests published openly to client desks.
  • Stewardship: Safe hydrometallurgy eliminating informal toxic dumping.
  • Compliance: Zero landfill diversion and uncompromising statutory adherence.
