About EcoGreen

A decade of operating history. One standard.

EcoGreen engineers advanced closed-loop recovery systems that extract 99.5% high-purity value from complex technological waste streams — safeguarding enterprise data while securing critical domestic raw materials.

Company overview

Who we are

EcoGreen is India's certified e-waste recycling and IT asset disposition partner, operating a network of authorised facilities across the country. Founded in 2015, we provide enterprise organizations with auditable chain-of-custody tracking, cryptographic data destruction, and manufacturing-grade hydrometallurgical mineral recovery.

Our facilities in Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Telangana bridge the gap between high-volume corporate hardware refresh cycles and domestic raw material security. We operate under zero-landfill mandates, converting complex electronic scrap into strategic industrial inputs.

Guiding principles

Mission, vision, and operational values.

01 / MISSION

Mission

To make e-waste a resource, not a liability — through closed-loop recovery that protects both enterprise data and the environment.

02 / VISION

Vision

A zero-landfill India where every device that reaches end-of-life returns its minerals to domestic manufacturing.

03 / VALUES

Values

  • Integrity: Unbroken serial chain of custody verified at every weighbridge.
  • Transparency: Audited recovery manifests published openly to client desks.
  • Stewardship: Safe hydrometallurgy eliminating informal toxic dumping.
  • Compliance: Zero landfill diversion and uncompromising CPCB adherence.
