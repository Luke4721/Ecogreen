ENTERPRISE STEWARDSHIP

Making The Impossible Possible in the Circular Electronics Industry

Founded in 2024, EcoGreen is India's statutory leader in certified e-waste recycling and IT asset disposition. We operate an integrated network of authorized processing plants bridging high-volume corporate refresh cycles with domestic raw material security.

Enterprise Security Meets Environmental Stewardship

Operating across Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Telangana, we provide enterprise organizations with auditable chain-of-custody tracking, cryptographic data destruction, and manufacturing-grade chemical material recovery.

We operate under zero-landfill mandates, converting discarded printed circuit boards, high-density storage arrays, and lithium battery cells into strategic industrial inputs for Indian manufacturers.

Our Philosophy

Guided by Our Strong Set of Core Values

The guiding principles that dictate how we handle client data, protect natural ecosystems, and empower our workforce every single day.

01 / INTEGRITY

Uncompromising Compliance

We uphold 100% adherence to Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) mandates and ensure zero landfill waste across every contracted load.

02 / SECURITY

Data Sanitization Obsession

Every magnetic platter and solid-state chip is purged under NIST 800-88 standards or physically pulverized with cryptographic serial certificates.

03 / INNOVATION

Closed-Loop Chemistry

We continually refine our proprietary chemical refining reagents to extract high-yield minerals at lower energy thresholds.

04 / EXCELLENCE

Operational Discipline

Operating under GreenCo Gold, ISO 9001, and ISO 14001 standards, our continuous improvement protocols minimize material loss at every station.

05 / AGILITY

Speed & Reliability

Rapid enterprise response with secure GPS-tracked fleet pickups completed within 48 to 72 hours across all major Indian metro clusters.

06 / STEWARDSHIP

Social Responsibility

Fair wages, zero child labor, and rigorous health safeguards for every floor technician and hazardous material handler.

07 / RESPECT

Transparent Governance

Full open-book chain of custody, auditable mass balances, and immediate Form 6 filing transparency for all corporate stakeholders.
