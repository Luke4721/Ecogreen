Core Capabilities

End-to-End Enterprise ITAD Operations

Engineered specifically for Tier-1 banking, cloud infrastructure, and large corporate IT departments.

Enterprise Server & Data Center Decommissioning

Complete turnkey de-racking, cabling disconnection, serial audit, and optical verification inside high-security raised-floor data center environments.

  • Dual-engineer custody signoff against master corporate asset records.
  • Live barcode verification and discrepancy logging before transit.
  • Dedicated electrostatic-discharge (ESD) safe packing and shock-proof transit cages.

Cryptographic Purge & Physical Destruction

On-site and off-site data destruction supporting both magnetic spinning disks and modern high-density NVMe / SSD flash storage architectures.

  • NIST 800-88 Rev. 1 compliant 3-pass and crypto-erase sanitization.
  • High-coercivity electromagnetic degaussing for magnetic tapes and platter drives.
  • 2mm cross-cut hydraulic shredding with video recording and legal certificates.

Armoured Reverse Logistics & Chain of Custody

Dedicated logistics fleet equipped with GPS tracking, remote electronic door locks, and CCTV surveillance connecting all major metros to our 6 authorized processing facilities.

  • Tamper-evident numbered security seals cross-checked at origin and destination.
  • Real-time geofenced tracking with instant alert protocols for route deviations.
  • Comprehensive transit insurance coverage underwritten by leading institutional insurers.

Component Harvesting & Circular Remarketing

Functional diagnostics to identify high-value enterprise components suitable for secondary lifecycles, reducing carbon overhead and generating cash rebate offsets for clients.

  • Enterprise CPU, ECC RAM, and enterprise power supply testing and grading.
  • Secondary lifecycle resale through vetted, authorized downstream partners.
  • Zero-landfill mandate ensuring 100% of non-viable hardware enters chemical refining.
