Sanitization Standards

NIST 800-88 Rev.1 Purge Explained

Why simple formatting fails enterprise audits — and how cryptographic erasure guarantees data finality.

Simple drive formatting or partition deletion leaves magnetic domains and flash NAND blocks vulnerable to forensic extraction. EcoGreen executes cryptographic erasure and multi-pass overwrite protocols certified to NIST Special Publication 800-88 Rev. 1 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization).

The Purge command executes internal drive controller routines that overwrite all addressable logical blocks, host-protected areas (HPA), and device configuration overlays (DCO) with pseudorandom binary sequences, followed by full bit-level verification.

Technical Specifications

  • Purge standard: NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 cryptographic wipe and multi-pass overwrite protocols.
  • Verification method: Sector-by-sector LBA hash validation across 100% of addressable logical blocks.
  • Certificate format: Tamper-evident PDF certificate citing serial number, drive model, and sanitization timestamp.

Frequently Asked Questions

ITAD & Data Security Queries

Key answers regarding our on-site sanitization, audits, and certificate issuance.

What is the difference between Clear, Purge, and Destroy? +
Clear applies logical techniques that sanitize data in all user-addressable storage locations against simple non-invasive recovery. Purge applies cryptographic or physical techniques that render target data recovery infeasible using state-of-the-art laboratory techniques. Destroy uses physical shredding or disintegration to completely eliminate the media.
Do you shred on our premises? +
Yes. Our mobile high-torque shredding units deploy directly to enterprise loading docks across major metros. Media is shredded into sub-2mm particles under security camera supervision with serialized scanning.
How long does a batch take? +
Standard on-site sanitization processes between 200 to 500 drives per day per mobile station. Off-site processing at our authorized facilities completes inventory triage and certification within 48 hours of intake.
Do you handle mixed asset types? +
Yes. We process heterogeneous enterprise consignments including server blades, SAN storage arrays, tape cartridges, laptops, smartphones, and networking switches under one unified manifest.
What happens to devices that still work? +
Working units undergo NIST 800-88 sanitization followed by diagnostic component testing. With client authorization, tested units are refurbished for certified secondary use or partner educational donation programs.
