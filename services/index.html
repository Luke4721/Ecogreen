Chain of custody

How it works

From the loading dock to the last gram of copper.

01

Collection & Logistics

Scheduled pickup or authorised drop-off. Every asset is tagged, weighed and scanned into the chain before it leaves your premises.

02

Sorting & Inventory

Manual triage separates reusable units from true end-of-life material. You receive a serialised inventory report within 48 hours.

03

Data Destruction

NIST 800-88 Rev.1 Purge wipe or on-site physical shredding, with a certificate of destruction issued per serial number.

04

Material Recovery

Metals and rare earths are separated through hydrometallurgical processes and routed to refiners who feed Indian manufacturing.

05

Refurbishment

Working devices are tested, repaired, securely wiped and rehomed through certified resale channels or partner NGO donations.

06

Responsible Disposal

Only what genuinely cannot be recovered is sent for authorised disposal, with signed downstream confirmation for every consignment.
