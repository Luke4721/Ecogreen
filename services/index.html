Chain of Custody

How It Works: From Loading Dock to Refined Ore

From the loading dock to the last gram of copper — an unbroken, auditable journey.

STEP 01

Collection & Logistics

Scheduled pickup or authorised drop-off. Every asset is tagged with a tamper-evident serialized barcode, weighed, and scanned into the chain before it leaves your premises.

STEP 02

Sorting & Inventory

Manual triage separates reusable units from true end-of-life material. You receive a serialized inventory reconciliation report within 48 hours of facility intake.

STEP 03

Data Destruction

NIST 800-88 Rev.1 Purge wipe or mobile on-site physical shredding, with a tamper-evident Certificate of Destruction issued per device serial number.

STEP 04

Material Recovery

Metals and rare earths are separated through closed-loop hydrometallurgical processes and routed to accredited refiners who feed domestic Indian manufacturing.

STEP 05

Refurbishment

Working devices are tested, repaired, securely sanitized, and rehomed through certified secondary channels or client-directed educational donation programs.

STEP 06

Responsible Disposal

Only what genuinely cannot be recovered is sent for authorized disposal, with signed downstream confirmation for every single consignment. Zero landfill diversion.
