Process Discipline

Collaborative Chain-of-Custody Lifecycle

A continuous, auditable 6-stage workflow ensuring zero data exposure and statutory Central Pollution Control Board compliance.

STAGE 01

Serialized Pre-Audit

On-site scanning and asset tagging against client manifests, verifying OEM serial numbers and storage drive counts before transit.

STAGE 02

Armoured GPS Logistics

Tamper-evident locked containers transported in dedicated GPS-monitored vehicles with active geofencing and dual-driver custody.

STAGE 03

Certified Data Destruction

NIST 800-88 cryptographic 3-pass erasure or 2mm hydraulic pulverization with camera-monitored video recording and certificate release.

STAGE 04

Mechanical Separation

Robotic disassembly, magnetic ferrous sorting, eddy-current non-ferrous separation, and optical sorting of populated motherboards.

STAGE 05

Hydrometallurgical Refining

Zero-smelting chemical extraction producing high-grade mineral ingots (99.5% pure) that feed directly into national manufacturers.

STAGE 06

Statutory CPCB Form 6

Automated delivery of Green Certificates, carbon offset metrics, and legal CPCB Form 6 documentation absolving all enterprise liability.
