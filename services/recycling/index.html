Chemical Engineering

The 4-Stage Chemical Refining Process

Ambient temperature, closed-loop chemical extraction replacing archaic smelting furnaces.

STAGE 01

Precision Mechanical Beneficiation

Electronic boards are sheared and pulverized in sealed rotary hammer mills equipped with HEPA filtration. Electrostatic and eddy-current separators divide metallic fractions from inert epoxy substrate dust.

STAGE 02

Selective Ambient Leaching

Concentrated metal powders undergo stage-specific acid and alkaline leaching in titanium reactors. Proprietary biodegradable chelating agents dissolve base and precious metals into pregnant leach solutions without off-gassing.

STAGE 03

Solvent Extraction & Electrowinning

Organic solvent extraction separates copper, nickel, and precious metals into pure ionic streams. High-efficiency electrowinning cells deposit 99.8% pure copper cathodes and precipitate gold sponge.

STAGE 04

Zero Liquid Discharge & Ingot Casting

Precipitated sponge metals are induction-melted into assay-stamped ingots. Process acids and wash waters pass through multi-effect evaporators and reverse osmosis filters, recycling 100% of liquid back into the circuit.

Zero Downstream Leakage

Strict Vetted Downstream Vendor Accountability

EcoGreen conducts semi-annual physical audits on 100% of secondary material recipients. Non-metallic fractions such as shredded flame-retardant polymers are co-processed exclusively inside authorized cement kilns as fuel substitutes, ensuring total thermal elimination with zero landfill residue.

Clients receive audited mass-balance certificates validating the exact end-state of every metric tonne consigned to our care.
