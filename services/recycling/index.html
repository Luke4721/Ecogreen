Industrial process

From mechanical dismantling to hydrometallurgy.

Traditional electronic waste recycling in the developing world relies on high-temperature pyrometallurgical incineration or uncontrolled acid washing. Both approaches emit hazardous dioxins, furans, and lead particulates into surrounding communities while suffering substantial precious metal loss.

EcoGreen's metallurgical processing complex in Mathura operates an advanced four-stage closed-loop pipeline. In Stage 1, decommissioned devices undergo mechanical dismantling, eddy-current separation, and magnetic sorting to segregate structural aluminium, steel, and high-grade engineered polymers.

In Stage 2, printed circuit boards and battery black mass enter closed hydrometallurgical leaching tanks. Mild chemical solutions selectively dissolve target metal ions into liquid phase at ambient atmospheric temperatures without thermal emissions.

In Stages 3 and 4, solvent extraction and selective electrowinning precipitate target elements sequentially. Pure cathode copper, gold sponge, and battery-grade cobalt salts are cast into certified commodities and returned directly to domestic Indian manufacturers.

Supply chain governance

Downstream vendor policy.

Our responsibility does not terminate at our facility gates. Every downstream smelter, chemical refiner, and polymer compounder that receives secondary fractions from EcoGreen operates under mandatory unannounced physical audit protocols.

Auditors inspect environmental emissions logs, wastewater treatment operations, worker safety gear, and weighbridge manifests. Any vendor discovered diverting materials toward informal scrap markets or failing zero-landfill verification faces immediate and permanent contract termination, accompanied by statutory notification to the State Pollution Control Board.

Material acceptance

Accepted and non-accepted materials.

✓ Accepted Streams

  • • Enterprise IT hardware (servers, storage, racks)
  • • Corporate laptop and desktop fleets
  • • Printed circuit boards (motherboards, RAM, cards)
  • • Telecom switches, routers, and base station units
  • • Smartphones, tablets, and handheld devices
  • • Lithium-ion battery packs and modules
  • • Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) units
  • • Insulated copper wiring and data cables
  • • Industrial electronic controllers and PLCs

✕ Not Accepted

  • • Radioactive or ionizing radiation equipment
  • • Biohazardous or hospital devices with biological fluids
  • • Mercury-containing thermometers and arc lamps
  • • Bare broken cathode-ray tube (CRT) funnel glass
  • • Unneutralized industrial acids and bulk liquid chemicals
  • • Municipal mixed garbage or organic domestic refuse
  • • Explosive military ordnance or munitions
