Privacy Policy

How EcoGreen handles corporate manifest data, personal information, and digital records.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

EcoGreen Recyclers (P) Limited collects organizational and personal contact information submitted through service inquiry forms, manifest schedules, pickup requests, and business communications. Information collected includes corporate names, business email addresses, contact telephone numbers, physical pickup locations, and serialized hardware inventory specifications.

2. Use of Information

Collected information is utilized strictly to coordinate logistical pickups, execute NIST 800-88 sanitized chain of custody, issue statutory Certificates of Data Destruction, file and CPCB Form 6 manifests. We do not monetize, sell, or license client data to third-party commercial brokers.

3. Data Sanitization & Client Media Confidentiality

Storage media received under ITAD agreements undergo certified cryptographic erasure or physical shredding in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1. No residual client data is extracted, archived, or retained in any format.

4. Retention & Statutory Disclosure

Statutory records, weight manifests, and Certificates of Destruction are retained for seven years in compliance with the E-Waste (Management) Rules 2016 and Indian corporate governance regulations.
